Mini Highland Cow · The Original Beer Steer

Wren.

"What guests have never seen in person, and what every guest will photograph."

Wren is what most guests have never seen in person, and what every guest will photograph. She is the original Wildbrook beer steer — fluffy, calm, and built for the slow circuit through a cocktail hour. She is the reason your reception ends up on someone's reel.

She wears a custom oak side-pack designed for tall cans and stemmed flutes. Her handler Margot walks her on a soft lead at the pace of a careful conversation. She does not startle. She does not hurry. She would, if pushed, beat any of us in a staring contest.

Reserve Wren early. She is in our most-requested rotation May through October, and we cap her schedule at ten events per month so the work stays good for her.
