02 — Why us

The standard, set higher.

We're a small team that obsesses over the details so your guests can stay in the moment.

01

TIPS-certified handlers

Every Wildbrook handler is alcohol-service certified in Washington. Trained for beer steers, weddings, and crowds.

02

Leave-no-trace

Our animals wear discreet collection bags. We arrive on a quiet schedule and leave the venue cleaner than we found it.

03

Custom dressing

Florals from your designer, lights, ribbon, monograms. The herd arrives styled for your day, not ours.

PNW born and raised

Each animal is sourced and raised within a 30-mile radius of Mt. Si. We know our valleys and we know our venues.
